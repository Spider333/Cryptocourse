A complete, university-grade course for Computer Science students on how Bitcoin, Ethereum, cryptocurrencies and blockchain technology work in depth. Taught at the [Faculty of Informatics and Information Technologies](https://www.fiit.stuba.sk/en.html?page_id=749) of the [Slovak University of Technology](https://www.stuba.sk/english.html?page_id=132) by [David Stancel, MSc.](https://davidstancel.com) of [CoinStory](https://coinstory.tech/) and Kristian Kostal, PhD.

The creation of this course has been supported by Ernst & Young. Thank you!

> **Free to use.** Use anything here for any purpose, as long as you credit the source. Comments, questions, topic ideas and resources to include are welcome at **david@coinstory.tech**.

## 1. Introduction to Digital Currencies and Blockchain

[![Slides for lecture 1](assets/badge-slides.svg)](https://slides.com/stancel/uni1)

**Topics**

- History and emergence of digital currencies: DigiCash, Bit Gold, b-money, Hashcash, RPOW, e-gold
- Fundamental technology stack
- Emergence of Bitcoin
- Main principles and definitions

**Reading & resources**

- [Hashcash](http://www.hashcash.org/papers/hashcash.pdf) by A. Back
- [Bit Gold](https://nakamotoinstitute.org/bit-gold/) by N. Szabo
- [b-money](http://weidai.com/bmoney.txt) by W. Dai
- [Bitcoin Whitepaper](https://nakamotoinstitute.org/bitcoin/) by S. Nakamoto
- [RPOW](https://nakamotoinstitute.org/rpow/) by H. Finney
- [The Byzantine Generals Problem](https://nakamotoinstitute.org/static/docs/the-byzantine-generals-problem.pdf) by L. Lamport, R. Shostak, M. Pease

</details>
